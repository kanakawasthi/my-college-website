A NOTE ABOUT MINDSPACE

Not a perfect-life app.
A more mindful moment.

MindSpace is a student-made wellbeing and self-reflection website concept. It offers simple activities for mood check-ins, journaling, mindful pauses and noticing everyday positives. It is not a medical service, does not diagnose conditions and cannot replace support from a qualified professional or a trusted person.
